KnowTheNext

A geography quiz for Apple TV. Free to start, with one purchase for everything else.

What it is

KnowTheNext shows you a country and asks you to name it. You might see its flag, its place on a map (with a magnifier for the smallest countries), its outline, its name or its capital, or hear its name read aloud. You answer by picking the right name, capital, flag or outline.

  • Play the whole World, or a group: Africa, Asia, Europe, Oceania, North America, South America, the Caribbean or the Pacific.
  • Make your own groups of countries.
  • Country names in 130+ languages.
  • Best scores to beat.

Free to start

The app is a free download. The Starter group, twelve well-known countries from every continent, is free to play with every clue, answer mode, difficulty and language.

One in-app purchase, the Unlock, opens every other group (all 250 countries and territories) and lets you make your own groups. You pay once; there are no subscriptions and no ads. With Family Sharing, everyone in your family gets it too. Bought it before, or on a new Apple TV? Use Restore Purchases in the app.

Get help

Found a bug, a wrong flag or name, or have an idea? Tell us here. You don't need an account.
