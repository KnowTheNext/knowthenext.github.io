Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-04

KnowTheNext is a geography quiz for Apple TV. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: nothing leaves your Apple TV.

What we collect

Nothing. KnowTheNext does not collect, send or share any personal data.

  • No analytics.
  • No ads.
  • No tracking, by us or by anyone else.
  • No accounts or sign-in.

What stays on your Apple TV

To remember your progress, the app keeps these on your Apple TV, in its local storage:

  • Your settings
  • Saved games
  • Custom groups you make
  • Your best scores

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app from your Apple TV deletes it.

Children

Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue at github.com/KnowTheNext/knowthenext.github.io/issues.
